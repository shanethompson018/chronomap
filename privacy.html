Chrono Map – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Chrono Map is a personal research tool: a map that moves through time, for recording people, places, events and the ties between them. It is published as a static web page and has no server of its own.

What data the app handles

Everything you enter (markers, positions, ties, sources, notes and photos) is stored on your own device in your browser's storage. Nothing you enter is sent to the app's author or to any third party.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, Chrono Map asks for permission to create and manage only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It uses this to keep a "Chrono Map" folder in your Drive containing your data file and your photos, so that the same information is available on each device you sign in from. The app cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.

Data goes directly from your browser to Google Drive. The app's author never receives it and has no access to your Google account. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the "Chrono Map" folder from your Drive to remove the synced copy.

Map tiles

Base maps are loaded from Esri and OpenTopoMap tile servers. Like any web map, those servers receive the ordinary web requests needed to deliver the map images (such as your IP address and the map area you are viewing). No data you entered in the app is sent to them.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Chrono Map sets no tracking cookies, uses no analytics and shows no advertising.
